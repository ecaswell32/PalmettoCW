Specializations

Areas Where I Can Help

Every challenge you're facing deserves thoughtful, evidence-based support. Here's where I specialize — tailored to adults navigating life's complexities.

cyclone

Anxiety & Stress

Racing thoughts, constant worry, physical tension — anxiety can feel all-consuming. We'll identify the roots of your anxiety and build practical strategies to quiet the noise and reclaim your calm.

eco

Depression & Low Mood

When everything feels heavy and motivation is nowhere to be found, it can be hard to see a way forward. Together we'll gently unpack what's underneath and find pathways back to meaning.

spa

Grief & Loss

Loss reshapes us. Whether it's a person, a relationship, a chapter of life, or an identity — grief deserves real space. I'll walk alongside you through the process with compassion and care.

handshake

Relationship Challenges

Patterns in relationships — with partners, family, friends, or yourself — can feel impossible to break. We'll work to understand the dynamics at play and build healthier, more fulfilling connections.

work

Workplace Challenges

sync

Substance Use & Habits

Struggling with substances or habits you want to change? Through Motivational Interviewing and a judgment-free approach, we'll explore your readiness for change and build meaningful momentum at your own pace.

About Your Therapist

Meet Kyle

LMHC & CEAP | Founder, Palmetto Counseling & Wellness

I know what it's like to sit in that chair. I've been a client myself — and I still remember the feeling of saying something out loud for the first time. Something lifted. I felt lighter. That experience is part of why I do what I do.

Hello! I'm a Licensed Mental Health Counselor (LMHC) and a Certified Employee Assistance Professional (CEAP). My practice is entirely online, offering you a highly accessible, convenient, and confidential space to focus on your well-being — from the comfort of your own home.

I specialize in working with adults navigating life's complexities: anxiety, depression, relationship challenges, grief, and substance use. With my unique background as a CEAP, I also bring extensive experience in workplace stress, career burnout, and professional difficulties.

My Approach

Warm. Engaging. Solution-Focused.

I'm not the kind of therapist who sits back and quietly nods. I'm actively in your corner — asking intentional questions, offering tools, and working toward real solutions alongside you.

01

You're Heard First

Our first sessions are focused on truly understanding you — your story, your goals, and what life looks like when things feel right. No rushing to "fix" before we understand.

02

Tools That Actually Work

Using evidence-based methods like CBT, Motivational Interviewing, and mindfulness — translated into practical, everyday language — we build a toolkit that's uniquely yours.

03

Real-World Action

Between sessions, I love giving collaborative "homework" — small, intentional steps that create real-world change, not just insight that stays on the couch.

Therapeutic Methods

How We'll Work Together

Every method I use is grounded in research and chosen for how it translates into real-life change. Here's what that looks like in plain language:

psychology

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

We look at the connection between your thoughts, feelings, and behaviors — and work to replace patterns that aren't serving you with ones that actually do.

forum

Motivational Interviewing

Not ready to change? That's okay. This gentle, collaborative approach helps you explore your own reasons for change — without pressure or judgment.

nature

Mindfulness Techniques

Practical, grounding tools to bring you back to the present moment — reducing overwhelm and building the resilience to face life's challenges.

What to Expect
When You Reach Out
